# Modules

Mission Operations Portal is organized as independent modules behind one authenticated workspace.

## Current Modules

- Help Desk: tickets, categories, notes, attachments, AI-assisted triage, and knowledge suggestions.
- IMS: inventory assets, assigned-user assets, exports, and audit history.
- Stock: stock catalog, requests, approvals, stock cards, movements, and reports.
- Digital Signature: internal signing envelopes, signing links, PDF stamping, verification, and audit trails.
- LAN Messenger: local office messaging, groups, channels, attachments, meetings, and call-session records.
- Reports and Dashboards: operational reporting and embedded dashboard placeholders.
- Admin Center: users, roles, permissions, modules, organization settings, and branding.

## Future Modules

- Procurement: request, approval, purchasing, supplier, and delivery workflows.
- Network Access: future UniFi-oriented access requests, approvals, device records, and audit logs.
- Microsoft 365/OneDrive sync folder option for document exchange where an organization enables it.
